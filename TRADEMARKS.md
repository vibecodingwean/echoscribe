## Forks and derived applications

Forks and derived applications intended for public distribution must be renamed and rebranded clearly enough to avoid confusion with the official EchoScribe application. A public third-party distribution should use its own distinct:

- product and application name;
- package, bundle, and extension identifiers where changing them is technically required to prevent confusion or update-line collisions;
- signing identity and publisher identity;
- logos, icons, screenshots, store descriptions, and other store presentation;
- website, support contact, and release metadata.

Without prior written permission from vibecodingwean, a derived application may not be published under the name “EchoScribe” or with the EchoScribe logo in Google Play, the Apple App Store, browser-extension stores, or any other application marketplace.

Third parties must not state or imply that their version is official, approved, sponsored, endorsed, or otherwise affiliated with the original EchoScribe project, vibecodingwean, or the official repository.

## Permitted factual references

Accurate, non-misleading statements such as “derived from the EchoScribe source code” are permitted when they describe the origin of the code and do not use the EchoScribe Marks as the derived product’s own name or branding. Such references must not suggest endorsement or an official relationship.
